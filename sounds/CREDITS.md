# Sound library: credits and licences

Every file in this folder is a recording of a real instrument taken from an openly licensed sample library and prepared for First Loop by `tools/build-sounds.py`: cut to the start of the note, shortened and faded, some samples retuned by resampling, held notes given a cross-faded loop, levels adjusted, drum microphones mixed to stereo, converted to MP3. Only libraries under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) or CC BY (attribution) are used; nothing here is under a non-commercial, no-derivatives or share-alike licence.

This file is generated from `manifest.json`; the same licence, source and attribution text is in each manifest entry (`licence`, `source`, `credit`).

## Attribution required

These two libraries are under CC BY 3.0: the lines below must stay with the files and be shown wherever the app lists its credits.

- **Grand piano**: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Samples were trimmed, level-adjusted and converted to MP3 for First Loop.
- **Electric piano (Wurlitzer)**: Wurlitzer EP200 electric piano samples by Greg Sullivan (http://www.sullivang.net/), licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Samples were trimmed, level-adjusted and converted to MP3 for First Loop.

## Looked at and not used

- jRhodes3c / jRhodes3d (Jeff Learman, Rhodes Mark I): the samples are CC BY-NC, not usable in a product. The electric piano here is the Wurlitzer instead.
- gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts: renders of the FluidR3_GM (MIT) and MusyngKite (CC BY-SA) soundfonts; share-alike was ruled out and real multi-sampled sources were preferred.
- nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments: a collection from mixed sources whose individual licences could not be confirmed.
- Nylon-string guitar: no clean permissively licensed source was found (the Discord SFZ GM Bank entry is an empty placeholder).
